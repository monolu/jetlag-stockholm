# Stockholm house rules

Six players, two teams of three, the whole SL rail network, base deck plus Expansion
Pack Vol. 1 with **every curse in**. The rulebook is written in miles for cities
that aren't built on islands, and it leaves plenty to the local group. These are our calls.
Read them out loud before round one.

---

## 1. Everything in metric

| Rulebook | We use |
|---|---|
| Hiding zone, ¼ mile | **400 m** radius from the stop |
| Caught: within 5 ft | **1.5 m** and recognised |
| Hiding spot within 10 ft of a path | **3 m** |
| Photo "5 ft × 5 ft section" | **1.5 × 1.5 m** |
| Photo of a park: stand 5 ft from obstructions | **1.5 m** |
| Radar ¼ / ½ / 1 / 3 / 5 / 10 mi | **400 m / 800 m / 1.6 km / 5 km / 8 km / 16 km** |
| Radar 25 / 50 / 100 mi | **40 km / 80 km / 160 km** (160 km is dead, see §6) |
| Thermometer ½ / 3 / 10 mi | **800 m / 5 km / 16 km** |
| Tentacle "within 1 mile" | **1.6 km** |
| Curse distances: 100 ft / 500 ft / 1,000 ft / ¼ mi / 5 mi | **30 m / 150 m / 300 m / 400 m / 8 km** |

Measure in Google Maps: long-press an empty spot → *Measure distance*.

## 2. Getting around

- **Legs and every SL rail line**: tunnelbana, pendeltåg, trams 7 / 12 / 21 / 30 / 31,
  Roslagsbanan and Saltsjöbanan. Nothing else moves anyone: no SL buses, no ferries, no
  Arlanda Express, no SJ or Mälartåg service, no taxi, no bike, no scooter, no lift from a
  friend.
- The plain SL ticket doesn't cover the Arlanda passage or the Uppsala stretch. Everyone
  buys those extensions before the game, because both are in play.
- Same rules for both teams.

## 3. Game hours, tickets, money

- **Play runs 09:00–18:00**, two rounds, one per team. A round still going at 18:00
  carries into the next day: everyone stops where they stand and starts from the same
  spot next morning, with every timer paused overnight.
- The metro runs roughly 05:00–01:00. The trams, Roslagsbanan, Saltsjöbanan and the
  outer pendeltåg branches all stop much earlier, so check the SL app before committing to
  a far-out zone.
- Tickets: single ~43 kr / 75 min, 24-hour ticket ~180 kr, in the SL app, plus the
  Arlanda passage and the Uppsala extension.
- "Publicly accessible during **all game hours**" is what binds hiding spots. Libraries,
  malls and museums shut at 17–18:00, so a spot inside one is illegal even at 13:00. Outdoors, station concourses and covered public passages are safe.
  The spot must also sit within 3 m of a path or road the map app would route someone along.
- Stockholm is effectively cashless. Everyone carries a card that works. Several curses
  cost money (an egg, lemons, an advertised product, a stamp) and we're keeping all of
  them, so budget a couple of hundred kronor per team.

## 4. Administrative divisions

| Rulebook | Sweden | In our map |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **Län** | Stockholms län holds 248 stops, Uppsala län 3 (Uppsala C, Knivsta, Bålsta), Södermanlands län 1 (Gnesta). Live, but lopsided. |
| 2nd | **Kommun** | 24 of them, Stockholm (120 stops) down to six kommuner with one stop each |
| 3rd | **Distrikt** | The admin level 9 area on OpenStreetMap: stadsdelsområde inside Stockholms kommun, distrikt everywhere else |
| 4th | **Stadsdel** | Stockholms kommun only. If either side is outside it, the matching answer is **no**. |

- The kommun of every stop is in `data/stations.csv`. Nobody looks anything up mid-round.
- Google Maps doesn't draw these borders. **OpenStreetMap is the source of truth** - drop a
  pin on openstreetmap.org and read the boundaries off it.

## 5. Landmasses and coastline

**Separate landmasses in our game:**

- **The mainland** - everything north and west of the water: Uppsala, Knivsta, Arlanda,
  Märsta, Sollentuna, Solna, Sundbyberg, Danderyd, Täby, Vallentuna, Österåker, Norrmalm,
  Vasastan, Östermalm, Gärdet, Bromma, Järfälla, Bålsta, and, round the west end of
  Mälaren, Södertälje west of the canal, Järna, Mölnbo and Gnesta.
- **Södertörn** - everything south and east of the water: Söderort, Huddinge, Botkyrka,
  Salem, Tumba, Haninge, Nynäshamn, Nacka, Saltsjöbaden and Östertälje. The Södertälje
  canal is the divide, so this is not the same landmass as the mainland.
- **Lidingö**
- **Kungsholmen** (with Stadshagen, Kristineberg, Marieberg), **Södermalm**,
  **Djurgården**, **Långholmen**, **Reimersholme**, **Stora Essingen**, **Lilla Essingen**
- **Stadsholmen** (Gamla stan), **Riddarholmen**, **Helgeandsholmen** - three islands, not one
- **Skeppsholmen + Kastellholmen**, counted as one

Blasieholmen is a peninsula. A bridge joins nothing.

**Coastline.** The rulebook's definition (a channel never narrower than 1.6 km) would make
none of Stockholm a coast, so we override it:

> **Coastline = the salt-water side.** Everything seaward of the Slussen and Hammarby
> locks: Strömmen, Nybroviken, Saltsjön, Lilla Värtan, Djurgårdsbrunnsviken, the Nacka and
> Lidingö shores, and the water out to Nynäshamn. Mälaren, Riddarfjärden, Årstaviken and
> all lakes are **not** coastline.

## 6. The questions that can't tell them anything

Three, and only three. They still count as asked and **still pay the hider cards**.

| Question | Why |
|---|---|
| International border | The nearest one is hundreds of kilometres outside the circle. Null. |
| High-speed train line | No line in the circle reaches 250 km/h on purpose-built track, and we don't count the 200 km/h stretches. Null. |
| Radar 160 km | The circle is 110 km across, so the answer is always yes. |

**Live, and worth saying out loud.** Three questions that a city-sized Stockholm map would
kill are real on this one:

- **Commercial airport** - Bromma and Arlanda are both inside the circle.
- **1st admin division** - three län, four stops outside Stockholms län.
- **1st admin division border** - the Uppsala län border crosses between Arlanda and
  Knivsta and again between Bro and Bålsta; the Södermanlands län border crosses between
  Mölnbo and Gnesta.

**Settled before the first round.** Four categories depend on how Google Maps labels
things, and Google and OpenStreetMap disagree badly across a map this size. Search each
inside the circle, write down what you find, and that list is binding; the five-review rule
decides anything doubtful.

- **Mountain** - OpenStreetMap marks 218 named hilltops in the circle, most 20-100 m high.
- **Amusement park** - Gröna Lund is certain; indoor playgrounds and folkparker are not.
- **Zoo** - Skansen is certain; the 4H farms and parklek animal pens are not.
- **Aquarium** - Skansen-Akvariet is the only clear one.

**Everything else works:** kommun, landmass, station name length, street or path, park,
museum, library, movie theater, hospital, foreign consulate, golf course, body of water,
sea level, coastline, rail station, transit line, every thermometer, radar 400 m to 80 km,
every photo, and all four tentacles.

## 7. Question rulings, Stockholm edition

- **Rail station (measuring)**: everything on rails inside the circle counts - our own
  stops, tram stops included, plus the SJ, Mälartåg and Arlanda Express stations we can't
  ride.
- **Station name length**: count the name exactly as it appears in `data/stations.csv`,
  spaces, hyphens and slashes included. Gamla stan = 10, T-Centralen = 11,
  Torsvik/Millesgården = 20.
- **Foreign consulate**: Google Maps' embassy and consulate category, honorary consulates
  excluded.
- **Transit line matching**: seekers must be on a moving train or tram, and they screenshot
  the full stop list when they ask.
- **Photo outside a station entrance** at a stop with no station building: stand at the
  platform or the stop pole.
- **Underground**: seeker tracking dies in the tunnels. For curse costs that depend on
  where the seekers are, use their last known position; if it's more than ten minutes
  stale, the hiders may ask outright and the seekers answer honestly.
- **Language**: pick Swedish or English before round one. It decides Hidden Hangman and
  Quill.

## 8. Time traps

Traps go on one of the 252 stops in `data/stations.csv` and nowhere else. Passing through
a station means travelling a line that physically runs through it.

## 10. Bits the rulebook doesn't say but we should

- Log every question, its answer, the time, every card drawn, and where the seekers were
  standing (Rewind needs that last one).
- Hiders: check your zone has a **toilet** and food before you commit. Tram stops on
  Lidingö and Bromma have neither. Malls, libraries, McDonald's and Pressbyrån do.
- Seekers: decide what you'll do with an answer before you spend the question. Every one
  hands them 3 to 18 minutes, or a share of the whole clock if they draw a per cent card.
- Everyone shares live location with someone who isn't playing.
- Anyone can call a pause. Timers stop, positions freeze, nobody argues.
