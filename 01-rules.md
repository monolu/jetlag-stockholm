# The rules, digested

Base game digested from <https://www.lifack.ch/docs/quick_start_guide>; expansion
mechanics from the official reference rules at
<https://rules.jetlagthegame.com/expansion/>. Medium-game numbers are baked in, because
that's what we're playing. Metric conversions are in `03-house-rules.md` — wherever this
says miles, use those.

## The shape of a round

1. One **team of three hides**, the other three band together as **seekers**.
2. Everyone starts in the same place. Hiders take the deck; seekers take the investigation
   book. Seekers' location sharing **on**, hiders' **off**.
3. **Hiding period: 60 minutes.** The hiding team travels on foot and on SL rail to
   any of the 235 stops. Wherever they stand at 60:00 is their zone, full stop.
4. The hiding clock starts. Seekers ask questions; hiders answer truthfully and draw cards
   for every answer.
5. When a seeker enters the zone on foot, the **end game** starts: the hiders freeze in
   their final spot.
6. **Caught** = a seeker within 1.5 m of any hider, and has recognised them. Clock stops.
   Time bonuses still in hand get added, fixed cards and per cent cards alike.
7. Ten minutes of transition, then the other team hides, starting from where this round
   ended.
8. **Longest single run wins.** Two rounds, one each. (If you have time for four, each
   team hides twice and only their best round counts — runs never add up.)

## Playing as teams of three

The rulebook is written for solo hiders and says team weirdness gets resolved case by
case. Our calls:

- **Hiders move as one.** All three stay together, within sight of each other, in one
  zone, and freeze in one hiding spot. No splitting up to make the seekers check three
  places.
- **Caught** applies to the whole team: a seeker who reaches 1.5 m of *any* hider and
  recognises them ends the round.
- One nominated hider phone answers questions, so answers can't contradict each other.
- **Photo of "You"** means all three of you in the frame. Selfie mode, arm extended, no
  zoom.
- **Seekers may split up.** They're still one team: one question at a time, one
  investigation book, and every curse applies to all three of them. The end game starts
  when the first of them walks into the zone.
- Curses that say "each seeker" now cost three times as much — three lemons, six litres of
  water. That's the hider's reward for being outnumbered in the round they're hunted.

## Hiding zone

- Centred on one of our 235 stops. If several stops sit inside your circle you still have
  to nominate *one* as "your" station: questions and photos refer to it.
- **500 m radius** around that stop's map icon.
- Move freely inside the circle until the end game.

## Final hiding spot

- **Publicly accessible during all game hours.** Not a toilet stall, not a flat, ideally
  not a shop, since shops close and staff move you on.
- Within **3 m of a path or road Google Maps would route you along**.
- Locked the instant a seeker crosses into your circle. Scout it early: a good spot is
  worth an hour after they've found your station.

## Questions

| Category | Format | Draw / keep | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Matching** | "Is your nearest ___ the same as mine?" | draw 3, keep 1 | 5 min |
| **Measuring** | "Compared to me, closer to or further from ___?" | draw 3, keep 1 | 5 min |
| **Thermometer** | "After travelling ___, am I hotter or colder?" | draw 2, keep 1 | 5 min |
| **Radar** | "Are you within ___ of me?" | draw 2, keep 1 | 5 min |
| **Photo** | "Send me a photo of ___." | draw 1, keep 1 | 10 min |
| **Tentacle** | "Within 1.6 km, which ___ are you nearest?" | draw 4, keep 2 | 5 min |

- One question at a time. Nothing new until the current one is answered.
- Repeats cost multiples: a second ask means the hider draws the reward twice, in two
  separate draws (3-keep-1, then 3-keep-1 — never 6-keep-2).
- Subject not inside the border → **null answer**, which still counts as asked and still
  pays the hider. Cross the permanently-null ones out of the book (`03-house-rules.md` §7).
- Missed window → the hiding clock **pauses** until they answer, and they draw nothing.
- **Radar** asks about the hiders' current location, not their zone.
- **Thermometer**: announce the start, send a pin, travel the distance as the crow flies,
  send a second pin.
- **Transit line matching** can only be asked from a moving vehicle. Screenshot the whole
  stop list and send it.
- Research: the entire internet **except Google Street View**.

## The deck

One hundred cards: **45 time bonuses, 25 powerups, 30 curses**. Hand limit **6** unless a
powerup raises it.

**Time bonuses.** The fixed cards carry three values, one per game size; we read the
middle one. The deck holds 9 red (3 min), 10 orange (6), 10 yellow (9), 3 green (12), 2
blue (18), 4 red per cent cards (5%), 2 orange per cent cards (10%), and 5 time traps.
Bonuses cannot be played, and they only count if they are in hand when the team is caught.
A per cent card is worth its share of the hiding time itself, worked out before any other
bonus is added, so they never compound with each other.

**Time traps.** Placed on one of our 235 stops and nowhere else. The card leaves your hand
the moment it is placed, and any time it wins is added at once. "Passing through" means
travelling a line that physically runs through that station.

**Powerups.** Discard 2 draw 3 (×4), discard 1 draw 2 (×4), randomize question (×4), veto
question (×4), duplicate another card (×2), discard me (×2), draw 1 expand 1 (×2), draw 2
expand 2 (×1), discard 3 draw 4 (×1), and **move** (×1) — discard your hand, reveal your
station, then 20 minutes to build a new zone anywhere on the map, seekers frozen and clock
paused, illegal during the end game.

**Curses.** Thirty of them, every one legal. Only one curse blocking questions or transit
runs at a time, the seekers announce when they have cleared it, and a spent curse is
discarded.

## Fair play, safety, the pause button

- Everyone shares location with a non-playing friend.
- Anyone can pause for safety, food or a bathroom emergency. Timers stop, positions freeze,
  and everyone resumes from exactly where they were.
- Hiders and seekers use the **same map app** (Google Maps) for anything involving a
  category of place.
- Google Maps category disputes: 5+ reviews means it's real, fewer than 5 means it isn't,
  unless everyone agrees otherwise.
- Seekers must clarify an ambiguous question *before* it's answered — screenshot what
  you're counting.
