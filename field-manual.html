Network

Lines and stops

Every SL rail line is in play: the tunnelbana, the pendeltåg, the trams, Roslagsbanan and Saltsjöbanan.

Nothing else carries anyone. No SL buses, no ferries, no Arlanda Express, no SJ or Mälartåg service, no taxi, no bike, no scooter, no lift from a friend. Both teams move on foot and on the lines below.

That is 100 tunnelbana stations, 60 tram stops, 40 pendeltåg stations, 36 on Roslagsbanan and 17 on Saltsjöbanan. Sixteen serve more than one system, which leaves 235 stops that can hold a hiding zone.

The stop counts above are the stops inside the border. Each line runs further in real life; those stations are not in the game. Where two stations of different systems sit within 300 m of each other they count as one stop and one zone: T-Centralen with Stockholm City, Tekniska högskolan with Stockholms östra, Sundbybergs centrum with Sundbyberg.

Map

The border

A circle of radius 28.7 km centred on 59.35 N, 17.92 E, a point near Sundbyberg. It covers 2,588 km², which is 999 square miles — just inside the 1,000 the rulebook allows a medium game.

That circle is the largest legal one, placed where it holds the most stops: 235 of the 252 on the network. What it leaves out is the far end of the long branches — Uppsala C, Knivsta, Arlanda, Märsta, Bålsta, Gnesta, Mölnbo, Järna, Nynäshamn and its neighbours, Kårsta, Ekskogen and Frösunda. None of them are in the game, and no ticket beyond a normal SL one is needed to reach anything that is.

Anything outside the circle does not exist. A question about it returns a null answer, which counts as asked and still pays the hiders their cards. A hiding zone may not reach past the border either: if part of a circle falls outside, that part is out of play.

Play

A round

  1. Before the start Handover The hiding team takes the deck, the seekers take the investigation book. Seekers switch their location sharing on; hiders switch theirs off. Both teams start in the same place.
  2. 60 minutes Hiding period The hiders travel on foot and on our lines to any of the 235 stops. Where they stand when the hour ends is their zone. The seekers stay put.
  3. From minute 60 The clock runs The seekers ask questions. The hiders answer truthfully within the window, then draw from the deck.
  4. Zone entered End game Once a seeker stands inside the 500 m circle and off transit, the hiders stop moving and stay in their final spot.
  5. Found Caught A seeker reaches 1.5 m of a hider and recognises them. The clock stops. Time bonuses still in hand are added.
  6. 10 minutes Swap The deck is reshuffled, the trackers change hands, and the next round starts from where the last one ended.

Each team hides once. The longest single run wins. Runs do not add up.

Hours

Play runs 09:00 to 18:00. A round still going at 18:00 carries on into the next day: everyone stops where they stand, notes the spot, and starts again from those same spots the next morning. Every timer stops overnight.

The zone

  • A zone is a circle of 500 m around one of the 235 stops.
  • If other stops fall inside the circle, the hiders name one as their station. Questions and photos refer to that one.
  • The hiders move freely inside the circle until the end game.
  • Two curses move that edge. Curse of the Prosperous Home adds half again, to 750 m. Curse of the Tiny Home halves it, to 250 m, and cannot be played once the end game has started. The map carries all three circles as separate layers.

The final spot

  • It must be somewhere the seekers can reach at any hour of the game. A toilet cubicle, a flat and a fenced yard are all out; so is anywhere that shuts before 18:00.
  • It must lie within 3 m of a path or road drawn on the map app, which means a path the app would use to give someone walking directions.
  • It is fixed the moment a seeker enters the circle. Wherever the hiders are standing then is where they stay.

Anyone may pause the game. Every timer stops, everyone stays where they are, and play resumes from the same spots.

How they work

  • One question at a time. The next one waits until this one is answered.
  • Asking the same question again costs double: the hiders draw the reward twice, in two separate draws, never as one larger draw.
  • A hider who misses the window pauses their own clock until they answer, and draws nothing for it.
  • A radar asks where the hiders are standing, not where their zone is.
  • A thermometer starts with a pin. The seekers travel the distance in a straight line, then send a second pin.
  • The transit line question can only be asked from a moving train or tram, and the seekers send the full stop list with it.
  • A tentacle question the hiders are out of range of is answered "not within reach", and still pays them.
  • Any research is allowed except Google Street View. The one exception is Curse of the Unguided Tourist, which supplies a Street View image.

Google Maps settles every category. Both teams search the same app, and a place with five or more reviews counts as real while a place with fewer does not, unless everyone agrees otherwise. The seekers screenshot what they are counting when they ask.

Definitions

What counts as what

Twenty matching questions, four tentacle questions, and what each one means here.

The rulebook's own wording is at lifack.ch. Where a question names a category of place, it means the category Google Maps puts it in, and distance is measured to the map icon rather than to the edge of the thing.

Land

These count as separate landmasses:

  • The mainland — everything north and west of the water: Solna, Sundbyberg, Danderyd, Täby, Vallentuna, Österåker, Norrmalm, Vasastan, Östermalm, Gärdet, Bromma, Järfälla, Sollentuna, Upplands Väsby, and round the west end of Mälaren to Södertälje west of the canal.
  • Södertörn — everything south and east of the water: Söderort, Huddinge, Botkyrka, Salem, Tumba, Haninge, Nacka, Saltsjöbaden and Östertälje. The Södertälje canal is the divide, so this is not the same landmass as the mainland.
  • Lidingö
  • Kungsholmen, which takes in Stadshagen, Kristineberg and Marieberg
  • Södermalm · Djurgården · Långholmen · Reimersholme · Stora Essingen · Lilla Essingen
  • Stadsholmen (Gamla stan), Riddarholmen and Helgeandsholmen, which are three islands and not one
  • Skeppsholmen and Kastellholmen, counted as one

Blasieholmen is a peninsula, not an island. A bridge does not join two landmasses.

Water

The coast is the salt water. Everything seaward of the Slussen and Hammarby locks counts as coastline: Strömmen, Nybroviken, Saltsjön, Lilla Värtan, Djurgårdsbrunnsviken, and the Nacka and Lidingö shores. Mälaren, Riddarfjärden, Årstaviken and the lakes do not.

The rulebook asks for a channel at least 1.6 km wide, which would leave Stockholm with no coast at all. The locks divide fresh water from salt, so we use them.

Other

  • Nearest rail station counts everything on rails inside the circle: our own stops, tram stops included, plus the SJ, Mälartåg and Arlanda Express stations we cannot ride.
  • A photo taken outside the station is taken at the platform or the stop pole when the zone has no station building.
  • Location sharing drops out in the tunnels. Curses that depend on where the seekers are use their last known position; if that is more than ten minutes old, the hiders may ask, and the seekers answer honestly.
  • The game language is fixed before the first round. It decides Curse of the Hidden Hangman and Curse of the Quill.
Deck

The deck

One hundred cards: time bonuses, powerups and curses, shuffled together. What is in it is the hiders' business.

A hider holds six cards unless a powerup raises the limit. Time bonuses cannot be played and count only if they are in hand when the team is caught. Only one curse that blocks questions or transit runs at a time, the seekers say when they have cleared it, and a spent card is discarded. A hider announces a powerup only when it changes something for the seekers.

Any information about where you started and ended the thermometer can be provided as normal, in the form of a location pin or text.
Curse Of The RewindIt is the responsibility of the hider to keep track of where the last question was asked (and therefore where the seekers must return before asking their next question.)
Curse Of The Right TurnThis curse only applies to street intersections, meaning the intersection between two roads intended for cars (or the pedestrian sidewalks along those roads.) This curse would not have any effect indoors, or in an area where there are no streets.
Curse Of The Runner“Running” is defined as a gait that does not have a double-support phase (i.e. both feet cannot be touching the ground at the same time.) If players want to be still and place both feet on the ground simultaneously, they must do so for 5 seconds or longer. Players may not discard part of a time bonus; they must discard cards in their entirety. It is possible that players may have to discard more than the required number of minutes in order to pay for this curse.
Curse Of The Seabird“Body of water” can be natural or manmade, but must be large enough to be visible on the mapping app you are using. “Off-transit” means that players have physically disembarked their last form of transit and that it has left the station (i.e. you cannot temporarily step off a train while it’s stopped to fulfill the condition.)
Curse Of The SharkPower-ups include veto, randomize, duplicate, discard 1 draw 2, discard 2 draw 3, discard 3 draw 4, expand maximum hand size by 1, and expand maximum hand size by 2.
Curse Of The Shrewd CriticIf the guessed location does not have a Google pin or any reviews, seekers may make another guess immediately. “Off-transit” means that players have physically disembarked their last form of transit and that it has left the station (i.e. you cannot temporarily step off a train while it’s stopped to fulfill the condition.)
Curse Of The SniperAll seekers must be visible in the photo provided. The 60 seconds begins from the moment the curse and photo are delivered.
Curse Of The Soothsayer“Triple rewards” means that the reward is earned three times in succession, not all at once (i.e. a radar question would have you draw two, keep one, draw two, keep one, draw two, keep one.)
Curse Of The Tiny HomeThis curse cannot be played if the hider would be outside their new zone once the curse takes effect; it must be played within a half radius of the station.
Curse Of The TricksterYou are not required to lie after playing this curse; you may choose to answer the next three questions truthfully.
Curse Of The U-Turn"Next station," in this context, refers to the next station that the seekers' current mode of transit will stop at; if there are stops along the line that their current route will skip, those should be disregarded. If you are not sure whether the seekers are on transit, or whether their route will stop at a particular station, you may ask them for that information. If there is any ambiguity, you should tell them what you believe their next station is when this curse is cast to confirm that you didn't misread your tracker. Even if the seekers' current mode of transit would eventually bring them closer to you, this curse may still be played so long as their next station is further; a line that heads in your direction but temporarily curves away is a particularly advantageous situation for this curse.
Curse Of The Unguided TouristThe human-built structure in question cannot be any part of a road, including curbs or sidewalks. If you are playing in a country or area with highly limited Google Street View coverage (such as Germany), this curse should be removed from the deck.
Curse Of The Urban ExplorerAny pending questions thta were asked on transit before this curse was played must still be answered. Questions can still be asked outside of transit stations, but seekers cannot be on a platform or in any building associated with the transit station when asking questions.
Curse Of The Zipped LipPower-ups include veto, randomize, duplicate, discard 1 draw 2, discard 2 draw 3, discard 3 draw 4, expand maximum hand size by 1, and expand maximum hand size by 2.
Curse Of The Zoologist"Bug" in this context refers to any insect, arachnid, diplopoda, chilopoda, or anything else that would be colloquially and commonly referred to as a "bug." "Wild" in this context means undomesticated and not kept in human captivity, including large-scale outdoor instances of captivity, such as farms or sanctuaries. The photo must include enough of the animal that it is recognizable within its category. If there is any dispute as to an animal's classification, defer to Wikipedia. Animals outside of any of these categories (such as crustaceans) cannot be used for this curse.
Curse Of Water WeightAny liquid already traveling with the seekers at the time that this curse is played (e.g. water bottles) does not count. The liquid can be in any number of containers, and can be passed back and forth between seekers at any time. The liquid can be set down when the seekers are stationary or on transit, but it is considered "abandoned" once it is no longer within 3 meters of any seeker. The hider must be informed of their bonus immediately. "Body of water" within this context does not necessarily mean natural, but it cannot be a pool and must be large enough to be marked on the map.
Discard 1, draw 2Playable at any time. It leaves the hand as it is played, so the hand ends the same size it started. It cannot be played without enough cards to discard.
Discard 2, draw 3Playable at any time. It leaves the hand as it is played, so the hand ends the same size it started. It cannot be played without enough cards to discard.
Discard 3, draw 4Playable at any time. It leaves the hand as it is played, so the hand ends the same size it started. It cannot be played without enough cards to discard.
Discard mePays a curse's whole casting cost on its own, as long as that cost is discarding cards.
Draw 1, expand 1Draws a card and raises the hand limit by one for the rest of the round. Two of them stack to eight.
Draw 2, expand 2Draws two cards and raises the hand limit by two for the rest of the round.
Duplicate another cardCopies any card in hand: curse, powerup or time bonus. The original stays in hand and can still be played. Left in hand at the end of a round it copies a time bonus, doubling it.
MoveDiscard the hand, tell the seekers the station, then 20 minutes to build a new zone anywhere on the map. The seekers are frozen and the hiding clock is paused for those 20 minutes, then it carries on from where it stopped. It cannot be played during the end game.
NothingDoes nothing. It cannot be played, and it cannot pay a casting cost.
Randomize questionPlayed in response to a question instead of answering. The seekers pick a different unasked question from the same category at random, by dice or generator, and that one is answered instead. The original question does not count as asked. A random pick that lands on a null answer still stands.
Time trapGoes on a station that is part of our network, one of the 235, and the seekers are told where. It leaves the hand as it is placed. Passing through means travelling a line that runs through that station.
Veto questionPlayed in response to a question instead of answering. The seekers are told a veto was used. The question still counts as asked, so asking it again costs the doubled price, and the hiders draw nothing for it.
