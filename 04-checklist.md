# Checklist & run sheet

Six people, two teams of three, two rounds, across the whole SL rail network. Each team hides once; longest run wins.

## The night before

- [ ] Everyone has read `01-rules.md` and `03-house-rules.md`. Not skimmed. Read.
- [ ] The My Maps map is built from `data/all-layers.kml`, **shared with all six**, and
      opened once in the Google Maps app on every phone.
- [ ] Decide and write down: start time, hard end time, start location, which team hides
      first (coin flip).
- [ ] Decide: game language Swedish or English (it decides Hidden Hangman and Quill).
- [ ] Verify in Google Maps: still only one amusement park, one zoo, one aquarium and no
      mountain inside the border? Write down what you find, it's binding all game.
- [ ] Deck shuffled: 45 time bonuses, 25 powerups, 30 curses. Every curse is in.
- [ ] **Buy stamps.** Curse of the Post Office needs a letter actually mailed, and hunting
      for stamps on a Sunday will eat an hour. Two per team, in a wallet.
- [ ] Everyone buys their SL ticket in the app. 24-hour ticket if it's a full day.
- [ ] Test location sharing between all six phones. Actually test it, it fails silently.
- [ ] Charge every phone and every power bank.
- [ ] Send someone who isn't playing your live locations.
- [ ] Check the weather, and the last departures on Roslagsbanan, Saltsjöbanan and the
      outer pendeltåg branches — they stop long before the metro does.

## In the bag

Per person:
- [ ] Phone, charged, with data
- [ ] Power bank + cable
- [ ] SL ticket in the app
- [ ] 2 × d6 dice (curses want them constantly)
- [ ] Water + food for at least half a day
- [ ] Weather layer, umbrella
- [ ] Card that works everywhere; Stockholm is cashless
- [ ] Pen and paper

Per team:
- [ ] Stamps (Post Office), a cup (Pong Champion), scrap paper (Featherless Flight, Labyrinth, Quill)
- [ ] The deck open on one phone (hiding team) or the investigation book (seeking team)
- [ ] `03-house-rules.md` open on someone's phone
- [ ] A shared note for the question / answer / card log

## Round run sheet

**T −10 min — handover**
- [ ] Hiding team takes the deck, reset and reshuffled
- [ ] Seeking team takes the investigation book, tick-boxes cleared
- [ ] All three seekers' location sharing **on**, all three hiders' **off**. Check each
      other's screens, don't take anyone's word for it.
- [ ] Both teams in the same place — round 1 at the agreed start, round 2 where the first
      hiders were caught

**T 0 — hiding period starts: 60 minutes**
- [ ] Start the hiding timer out loud
- [ ] Hiders leave together and stay together. Seekers stay put.
- [ ] Pick a stop you can actually reach with slack. Where the team stands at 60:00 is the
      zone, no take-backs.

**T +60 — the hunt**
- [ ] Start the hiding clock. This is the number that wins.
- [ ] Hiders: scout the zone. Toilet, food, final spot, and take the annoying photos early.
- [ ] Seekers: one question at a time. Decide what you'll do with the answer *before* you
      ask. Every question hands them 3–18 minutes.
- [ ] Hiders: answer inside the window (5 min, 10 min for photos), then draw.
- [ ] Time traps go on network stations only, and the funnel stations (T-Centralen, Gamla
      stan, Slussen, Alvik, Gullmarsplan) are where they actually catch people.

**End game**
- [ ] The moment any seeker is inside the 500 m circle and off transit, all three hiders
      freeze in their final spot. Say it in the chat when it happens.
- [ ] Caught = a seeker within 1.5 m of any hider and has recognised them
- [ ] Stop the clock. Add the time bonuses still in hand, then the per cent cards as a
      share of the clock itself.

**After**
- [ ] Log the final time, the station, the spot
- [ ] Ten minutes: reshuffle, swap roles, flip the trackers, breathe
