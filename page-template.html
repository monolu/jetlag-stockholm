Network

Lines and stops

Every SL rail line is in play: the tunnelbana, the pendeltåg, the trams, Roslagsbanan and Saltsjöbanan.

Nothing else carries anyone. No SL buses, no ferries, no Arlanda Express, no SJ or Mälartåg service, no taxi, no bike, no scooter, no lift from a friend. Both teams move on foot and on the lines below.

That is 100 tunnelbana stations, 60 tram stops, 40 pendeltåg stations, 36 on Roslagsbanan and 17 on Saltsjöbanan. Sixteen serve more than one system, which leaves 235 stops that can hold a hiding zone.

The stop counts above are the stops inside the border. Each line runs further in real life; those stations are not in the game. Where two stations of different systems sit within 300 m of each other they count as one stop and one zone: T-Centralen with Stockholm City, Tekniska högskolan with Stockholms östra, Sundbybergs centrum with Sundbyberg.

Map

The border

A circle of radius 28.7 km centred on 59.35 N, 17.92 E, a point near Sundbyberg. It covers 2,588 km², which is 999 square miles — just inside the 1,000 the rulebook allows a medium game.

That circle is the largest legal one, placed where it holds the most stops: 235 of the 252 on the network. What it leaves out is the far end of the long branches — Uppsala C, Knivsta, Arlanda, Märsta, Bålsta, Gnesta, Mölnbo, Järna, Nynäshamn and its neighbours, Kårsta, Ekskogen and Frösunda. None of them are in the game, and no ticket beyond a normal SL one is needed to reach anything that is.

Anything outside the circle does not exist. A question about it returns a null answer, which counts as asked and still pays the hiders their cards. A hiding zone may not reach past the border either: if part of a circle falls outside, that part is out of play.

Play

A round

  1. Before the start Handover The hiding team takes the deck, the seekers take the investigation book. Seekers switch their location sharing on; hiders switch theirs off. Both teams start in the same place.
  2. 60 minutes Hiding period The hiders travel on foot and on our lines to any of the 235 stops. Where they stand when the hour ends is their zone. The seekers stay put.
  3. From minute 60 The clock runs The seekers ask questions. The hiders answer truthfully within the window, then draw from the deck.
  4. Zone entered End game Once a seeker stands inside the 500 m circle and off transit, the hiders stop moving and stay in their final spot.
  5. Found Caught A seeker reaches 1.5 m of a hider and recognises them. The clock stops. Time bonuses still in hand are added.
  6. 10 minutes Swap The deck is reshuffled, the trackers change hands, and the next round starts from where the last one ended.

Each team hides once. The longest single run wins. Runs do not add up.

Hours

Play runs 09:00 to 18:00. A round still going at 18:00 carries on into the next day: everyone stops where they stand, notes the spot, and starts again from those same spots the next morning. Every timer stops overnight.

The zone

  • A zone is a circle of 500 m around one of the 235 stops.
  • If other stops fall inside the circle, the hiders name one as their station. Questions and photos refer to that one.
  • The hiders move freely inside the circle until the end game.
  • Two curses move that edge. Curse of the Prosperous Home adds half again, to 750 m. Curse of the Tiny Home halves it, to 250 m, and cannot be played once the end game has started. The map carries all three circles as separate layers.

The final spot

  • It must be somewhere the seekers can reach at any hour of the game. A toilet cubicle, a flat and a fenced yard are all out; so is anywhere that shuts before 18:00.
  • It must lie within 3 m of a path or road drawn on the map app, which means a path the app would use to give someone walking directions.
  • It is fixed the moment a seeker enters the circle. Wherever the hiders are standing then is where they stay.

Anyone may pause the game. Every timer stops, everyone stays where they are, and play resumes from the same spots.

How they work

  • One question at a time. The next one waits until this one is answered.
  • Asking the same question again costs double: the hiders draw the reward twice, in two separate draws, never as one larger draw.
  • A hider who misses the window pauses their own clock until they answer, and draws nothing for it.
  • A radar asks where the hiders are standing, not where their zone is.
  • A thermometer starts with a pin. The seekers travel the distance in a straight line, then send a second pin.
  • The transit line question can only be asked from a moving train or tram, and the seekers send the full stop list with it.
  • A tentacle question the hiders are out of range of is answered "not within reach", and still pays them.
  • Any research is allowed except Google Street View. The one exception is Curse of the Unguided Tourist, which supplies a Street View image.

Google Maps settles every category. Both teams search the same app, and a place with five or more reviews counts as real while a place with fewer does not, unless everyone agrees otherwise. The seekers screenshot what they are counting when they ask.

Definitions

What counts as what

Twenty matching questions, four tentacle questions, and what each one means here.

The rulebook's own wording is at lifack.ch. Where a question names a category of place, it means the category Google Maps puts it in, and distance is measured to the map icon rather than to the edge of the thing.

Transit

QuestionWhat it means here
Commercial airportAn airport with flights you can find on Google Flights. Bromma is the only one inside the border, so the matching version is always yes. The measuring version works and pulls west.
Transit lineYes if the train or tram the seekers are riding would stop at the hiders' station. They must be aboard and moving, and they send the full stop list. Twenty-one lines are in play.
Station's name lengthCharacters in the station name, spaces, hyphens and slashes included, spelled as in the table at the foot of this page. Gamla stan is 10, T-Centralen is 11, Torsvik/Millesgården is 20.
Street or pathThe nearest named street or path. A street ends where its name changes, so Sankt Eriksgatan stops being itself at the bridge; an unnamed path ends at an intersection.

Land

These count as separate landmasses:

  • The mainland — everything north and west of the water: Solna, Sundbyberg, Danderyd, Täby, Vallentuna, Österåker, Norrmalm, Vasastan, Östermalm, Gärdet, Bromma, Järfälla, Sollentuna, Upplands Väsby, and round the west end of Mälaren to Södertälje west of the canal.
  • Södertörn — everything south and east of the water: Söderort, Huddinge, Botkyrka, Salem, Tumba, Haninge, Nacka, Saltsjöbaden and Östertälje. The Södertälje canal is the divide, so this is not the same landmass as the mainland.
  • Lidingö
  • Kungsholmen, which takes in Stadshagen, Kristineberg and Marieberg
  • Södermalm · Djurgården · Långholmen · Reimersholme · Stora Essingen · Lilla Essingen
  • Stadsholmen (Gamla stan), Riddarholmen and Helgeandsholmen, which are three islands and not one
  • Skeppsholmen and Kastellholmen, counted as one

Blasieholmen is a peninsula, not an island. A bridge does not join two landmasses.

Water

The coast is the salt water. Everything seaward of the Slussen and Hammarby locks counts as coastline: Strömmen, Nybroviken, Saltsjön, Lilla Värtan, Djurgårdsbrunnsviken, and the Nacka and Lidingö shores. Mälaren, Riddarfjärden, Årstaviken and the lakes do not.

The rulebook asks for a channel at least 1.6 km wide, which would leave Stockholm with no coast at all. The locks divide fresh water from salt, so we use them.

Other

  • Nearest rail station counts everything on rails inside the circle: our own stops, tram stops included, plus the SJ, Mälartåg and Arlanda Express stations we cannot ride.
  • A photo taken outside the station is taken at the platform or the stop pole when the zone has no station building.
  • Location sharing drops out in the tunnels. Curses that depend on where the seekers are use their last known position; if that is more than ten minutes old, the hiders may ask, and the seekers answer honestly.
  • The game language is fixed before the first round. It decides Curse of the Hidden Hangman and Curse of the Quill.
Deck

The deck

One hundred cards: time bonuses, powerups and curses, shuffled together. What is in it is the hiders' business.

A hider holds six cards unless a powerup raises the limit. Time bonuses cannot be played and count only if they are in hand when the team is caught. Only one curse that blocks questions or transit runs at a time, the seekers say when they have cleared it, and a spent card is discarded. A hider announces a powerup only when it changes something for the seekers.
