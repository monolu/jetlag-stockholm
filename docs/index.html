Nothing else carries anyone: no SL buses, no ferries, no Arlanda Express, no SJ or Mälartåg, no taxi, bike, scooter or lift. Legs and these lines, both teams.

The stop counts are the stops inside the border; each line runs further in real life and those stations are out of the game. Where two stations of different systems sit within 300 m they count as one stop: T-Centralen with Stockholm City, Tekniska högskolan with Stockholms östra, Sundbybergs centrum with Sundbyberg. That leaves 235 stops that can hold a zone.

Border

The border

A circle of radius 28.7 km centred on 59.35 N, 17.92 E, near Sundbyberg. It covers 2,588 km², which is 999 square miles, just inside the 1,000 a medium game allows, and it is placed to hold the most stops.

Outside it are the ends of the long branches: Uppsala C, Knivsta, Arlanda, Märsta, Bålsta, Gnesta, Mölnbo, Järna, the Nynäshamn tail, Kårsta, Ekskogen and Frösunda. A normal SL ticket covers everything inside.

Anything outside the circle does not exist: a question about it answers null, which counts as asked and still pays the hiders. A zone may not reach past the border either. If part of a circle falls outside, that part is out of play.

Play

A round

  1. Before the start Handover The hiding team takes the deck, the seekers take the investigation book. Seekers switch location sharing on, hiders switch theirs off. Both teams start in the same place.
  2. 60 minutes Hiding period The hiders travel to any of the 235 stops. Wherever they are when the hour ends, they must be inside that stop's zone. The seekers stay put.
  3. From minute 60 The clock runs The seekers ask questions. The hiders answer truthfully inside the window, then draw.
  4. Zone entered End game Once a seeker stands inside the circle and off transit, the hiders stop moving.
  5. Found Caught A seeker reaches 1.5 m of a hider and recognises them. The clock stops and the time bonuses in hand are added.
  6. 10 minutes Swap Reshuffle, change the trackers over, and start the next round from here.

Each team hides once. The longest single run wins; runs do not add up. Play runs 09:00 to 18:00 and a round still going at 18:00 carries into the next day, everyone starting from the spot they stopped on, with every timer paused overnight. Anyone may pause the game at any time on the same terms.

The zone

  • A circle of 500 m around one of the 235 stops. Two curses change that radius, see numbers.
  • If other stops fall inside it, the hiders name one as their station. Questions and photos refer to that one.
  • The hiders move freely inside the circle until the end game.

The final spot

  • Somewhere the seekers can reach at any hour of the game: no toilet cubicle, no flat, no fenced yard, nothing that shuts before 18:00.
  • Within 3 m of a path or road the map app would route someone along.
  • Fixed the moment a seeker enters the circle. Wherever the hiders stand then is where they stay.

  • One question at a time. The next waits until this one is answered.
  • Asking the same question again costs double: the hiders draw its reward twice, in two separate draws.
  • A hider who misses the window pauses their own clock until they answer, and draws nothing for it.
  • A radar asks where the hiders stand, not where their zone is.
  • A thermometer starts with a pin, then a second pin after the distance, measured straight.
  • The transit line question is asked from a moving train or tram, with the full stop list sent alongside.
  • A tentacle out of range is answered "not within reach", and still pays.
  • All research is allowed except Google Street View.

Google Maps settles every category. Both teams search the same app. A place with five or more reviews counts, one with fewer does not, unless everyone agrees otherwise. The seekers screenshot what they count when they ask.

Land

Separate landmasses, and a bridge joins none of them:

  • The mainland, north and west of the water: Solna, Sundbyberg, Danderyd, Täby, Vallentuna, Österåker, Norrmalm, Vasastan, Östermalm, Gärdet, Bromma, Järfälla, Sollentuna, Upplands Väsby, and round the west end of Mälaren to Södertälje west of the canal.
  • Södertörn, south and east of it: Söderort, Huddinge, Botkyrka, Salem, Haninge, Nacka, Saltsjöbaden, Östertälje. The Södertälje canal divides the two.
  • Lidingö · Kungsholmen, with Stadshagen, Kristineberg and Marieberg · Södermalm · Djurgården · Långholmen · Reimersholme · Stora Essingen · Lilla Essingen
  • Stadsholmen (Gamla stan), Riddarholmen and Helgeandsholmen, three islands and not one. Blasieholmen is a peninsula.
  • Skeppsholmen and Kastellholmen, counted as one.

Water

The coast is the salt water, and it reaches the play area on two fronts. East of Stockholm, everything seaward of the Slussen, Norrström and Hammarby locks: Strömmen, Nybroviken, Saltsjön, Lilla Värtan, Djurgårdsbrunnsviken and the Nacka, Lidingö and Saltsjöbaden shores. South of the Södertälje lock: the canal below it, Igelstaviken and Hallsfjärden. Island shores in that water count too. Mälaren does not, nor its arms — Riddarfjärden, Årstaviken, Ulvsundasjön, Hammarby sjö — nor Brunnsviken, Edsviken or any lake.

The rulebook asks for a channel that never narrows below 1.6 km. No route from Stockholm to the open Baltic manages that: Oxdjupet and the Vaxholm channel are a few hundred metres across, Baggensstäket a few dozen. Read strictly there is no coast here at all, so we take the salt water instead.

Other

  • Nearest rail station counts everything on rails inside the circle, tram stops included, plus the SJ, Mälartåg and Arlanda Express stations we cannot ride.
  • A photo taken outside the station is taken at the platform or the stop pole where there is no station building.
  • Tracking drops out in the tunnels. Curses that turn on where the seekers are use their last known position; if it is over ten minutes old the hiders may ask, and the seekers answer honestly.
  • The game language is fixed before the first round. It decides Curse of the Hidden Hangman and Curse of the Quill.
Deck

The deck

One hundred cards. A hider holds six unless a powerup raises the limit. Time bonuses cannot be played and count only if they are in hand when the team is caught. One curse that blocks questions or transit runs at a time and the seekers say when they have cleared it. A spent card is discarded, and a powerup is announced only when it changes something for the seekers.

Curse Of Water WeightAny liquid already traveling with the seekers at the time that this curse is played (e.g. water bottles) does not count. The liquid can be in any number of containers, and can be passed back and forth between seekers at any time. The liquid can be set down when the seekers are stationary or on transit, but it is considered "abandoned" once it is no longer within 3 meters of any seeker. The hider must be informed of their bonus immediately. "Body of water" within this context does not necessarily mean natural, but it cannot be a pool and must be large enough to be marked on the map.
Discard 1, draw 2Playable at any time. It leaves the hand as it is played, so the hand ends the same size it started. It cannot be played without enough cards to discard.
Discard 2, draw 3Playable at any time. It leaves the hand as it is played, so the hand ends the same size it started. It cannot be played without enough cards to discard.
Discard 3, draw 4Playable at any time. It leaves the hand as it is played, so the hand ends the same size it started. It cannot be played without enough cards to discard.
Discard mePays a curse's whole casting cost on its own, as long as that cost is discarding cards.
Draw 1, expand 1Draws a card and raises the hand limit by one for the rest of the round. Two of them stack to eight.
Draw 2, expand 2Draws two cards and raises the hand limit by two for the rest of the round.
Duplicate another cardCopies any card in hand: curse, powerup or time bonus. The original stays in hand and can still be played. Left in hand at the end of a round it copies a time bonus, doubling it.
MoveDiscard the hand, tell the seekers the station, then 20 minutes to build a new zone anywhere on the map. The seekers are frozen and the hiding clock is paused for those 20 minutes, then it carries on from where it stopped. It cannot be played during the end game.
NothingDoes nothing. It cannot be played, and it cannot pay a casting cost.
Randomize questionPlayed in response to a question instead of answering. The seekers pick a different unasked question from the same category at random, by dice or generator, and that one is answered instead. The original question does not count as asked. A random pick that lands on a null answer still stands.
Time trapGoes on a station that is part of our network, one of the 235, and the seekers are told where. It leaves the hand as it is placed. Passing through means travelling a line that runs through that station.
Veto questionPlayed in response to a question instead of answering. The seekers are told a veto was used. The question still counts as asked, so asking it again costs the doubled price, and the hiders draw nothing for it.
