Network

Lines and stops

Every SL rail line is in play: the tunnelbana, the pendeltåg, the trams, Roslagsbanan and Saltsjöbanan.

Nothing else carries anyone. No SL buses, no ferries, no Arlanda Express, no SJ or Mälartåg service, no taxi, no bike, no scooter, no lift from a friend. Both teams move on foot and on the lines below.

Counted as places rather than as lines: 100 tunnelbana stations, 60 tram stops, 54 pendeltåg stations, 39 on Roslagsbanan and 17 on Saltsjöbanan. Sixteen of those serve more than one system, which leaves 252 stops that can hold a hiding zone.

Where two stations sit within 300 m of each other and belong to different systems, they count as one stop and one zone. That covers T-Centralen with Stockholm City, Tekniska högskolan with Stockholms östra, and Sundbybergs centrum with Sundbyberg.

Line 43X is an express variant of 43. It calls at no station 43 misses, so it counts as line 43. Numbers and colours follow SL's own rail network map, and no number is used twice, so the number alone names the line.

Map

The border

A circle of radius 55 km centred on 59.38 N, 17.79 E, a point near Barkarby. It covers about 9,500 km² and holds every stop in the game with a kilometre to spare. The furthest out are Uppsala C in the north and Gröndalsviken in the south, both about 54 km from the centre.

Anything outside the circle does not exist. A question about it returns a null answer, which counts as asked and still pays the hiders their cards.

The circle takes in Uppsala, Knivsta, Arlanda, Bålsta, Gnesta, Nynäshamn, Saltsjöbaden, Österskär and Kårsta. It leaves out Nyköping, Västerås, Enköping, Norrtälje and the outer archipelago.

Play

A round

  1. Before the start Handover The hiding team takes the deck, the seekers take the investigation book. Seekers switch their location sharing on; hiders switch theirs off. Both teams start in the same place.
  2. 60 minutes Hiding period The hiders travel on foot and on our lines to any of the 252 stops. Where they stand when the hour ends is their zone. The seekers stay put.
  3. From minute 60 The clock runs The seekers ask questions. The hiders answer truthfully within the window, then draw from the deck.
  4. Zone entered End game Once a seeker stands inside the 400 m circle and off transit, the hiders stop moving and stay in their final spot.
  5. Found Caught A seeker reaches 1.5 m of a hider and recognises them. The clock stops. Time bonuses still in hand are added, fixed cards and per cent cards alike.
  6. 10 minutes Swap The deck is reshuffled, the trackers change hands, and the next round starts from where the last one ended.

Each team hides once. The longest single run wins. Runs do not add up.

Hours

Play runs 09:00 to 18:00. A round that is still going at 18:00 carries on into the next day: everyone stops where they stand, notes the spot, and starts again from those same spots the next morning. Every timer stops overnight.

The zone

  • A zone is a circle of 400 m around one of the 252 stops.
  • If other stops fall inside the circle, the hiders name one as their station. Questions and photos refer to that one.
  • The hiders move freely inside the circle until the end game.

The final spot

  • It must be somewhere the seekers can reach at any hour of the game. A toilet cubicle, a flat and a fenced yard are all out; so is anywhere that shuts before 18:00.
  • It must lie within 3 m of a path or road drawn on the map app, which means a path the app would use to give someone walking directions.
  • It is fixed the moment a seeker enters the circle. Wherever the hiders are standing then is where they stay.

Anyone may pause the game. Every timer stops, everyone stays where they are, and play resumes from the same spots.

Questions

What the seekers can ask

CategoryFormHiders drawWindow
Matching"Is your nearest ___ the same as mine?"3, keep 15 min
Measuring"Compared to me, closer to or further from ___?"3, keep 15 min
Thermometer"After travelling ___, am I hotter or colder?"2, keep 15 min
Radar"Are you within ___ of me?"2, keep 15 min
Photo"Send me a photo of ___."1, keep 110 min
Tentacle"Within 1.6 km, which ___ are you nearest?"4, keep 25 min
  • One question at a time. The next one waits until this one is answered.
  • Asking the same question again costs double: the hiders draw the reward twice, in two separate draws, never as one larger draw.
  • A hider who misses the window pauses their own clock until they answer, and draws nothing for it.
  • A radar asks where the hiders are standing, not where their zone is.
  • A thermometer starts with a pin. The seekers travel the distance in a straight line, then send a second pin.
  • The transit line question can only be asked from a moving train or tram, and the seekers send the full stop list with it.
  • Both teams use Google Maps. A place with five or more reviews counts as real; a place with fewer does not, unless everyone agrees otherwise.
  • Any research is allowed except Google Street View. The one exception is Curse of the Unguided Tourist, which supplies a Street View image.
Dead

Questions that do nothing here

Three questions cannot tell the seekers anything on this map. They still count as asked, and the hiders still draw their cards.

QuestionWhy
International borderThe nearest one lies hundreds of kilometres outside the circle, so the answer is null wherever both teams stand.
High-speed train lineNo line in the circle reaches 250 km/h on purpose-built track. We count none of the 200 km/h stretches, so the answer is null.
Radar 160 kmThe circle is 110 km across, so the answer is always yes.

Live, and worth saying out loud

Three questions that a smaller Stockholm map would kill are real here:

  • Commercial airport. Two are in the circle, Bromma and Arlanda, so matching and measuring both work.
  • 1st administrative division. The map covers three län. Four stops sit outside Stockholms län: Uppsala C, Knivsta and Bålsta in Uppsala län, and Gnesta in Södermanlands län.
  • 1st administrative division border. The Uppsala län border crosses the line between Arlanda and Knivsta, and again between Bro and Bålsta. The Södermanlands län border crosses between Mölnbo and Gnesta.

Everything else works: kommun, landmass, station name length, street or path, park, museum, library, cinema, hospital, consulate, golf course, body of water, sea level, coastline, rail station, transit line, every thermometer, radars from 400 m to 80 km, every photo, and all four tentacle questions.

Settled before the first round

Four categories depend on how Google Maps labels things, and Google and OpenStreetMap disagree across a map this size. Search each one inside the circle before the game and write down what you find. That list is then binding, and the five-review rule decides anything doubtful.

  • Mountain — OpenStreetMap marks 218 named hilltops in the circle, most of them 20 to 100 m high. Google marks far fewer.
  • Amusement park — Gröna Lund is certain. Indoor playgrounds and folkparker sit on the line.
  • Zoo — Skansen is certain. The 4H farms and parklek animal pens sit on the line.
  • Aquarium — Skansen-Akvariet is the only clear one.

Land

These count as separate landmasses:

  • The mainland — everything north and west of the water, from Uppsala and Arlanda down through Stockholm's northern half and Bromma, then round the west end of Mälaren to Södertälje west of the canal, Järna, Mölnbo and Gnesta.
  • Södertörn — everything south and east of the water: Söderort, Huddinge, Botkyrka, Salem, Tumba, Haninge, Nynäshamn, Nacka, Saltsjöbaden and Östertälje. The Södertälje canal is the divide, so this is not the same landmass as the mainland.
  • Lidingö
  • Kungsholmen, which takes in Stadshagen, Kristineberg and Marieberg
  • Södermalm · Djurgården · Långholmen · Reimersholme · Stora Essingen · Lilla Essingen
  • Stadsholmen (Gamla stan), Riddarholmen and Helgeandsholmen, which are three islands and not one
  • Skeppsholmen and Kastellholmen, counted as one

Blasieholmen is a peninsula, not an island. A bridge does not join two landmasses.

Water

The coast is the salt water. Everything seaward of the Slussen and Hammarby locks counts as coastline: Strömmen, Nybroviken, Saltsjön, Lilla Värtan, Djurgårdsbrunnsviken, the Nacka and Lidingö shores, and the water out to Nynäshamn. Mälaren, Riddarfjärden, Årstaviken and the lakes do not.

The rulebook asks for a channel at least 1.6 km wide, which would leave Stockholm with no coast at all. The locks divide fresh water from salt, so we use them.

Other

  • Nearest rail station counts everything on rails inside the circle: our own stops, tram stops included, plus the SJ, Mälartåg and Arlanda Express stations we cannot ride.
  • Station name length counts the name as written in the tables below, spaces, hyphens and slashes included. Gamla stan is 10, T-Centralen is 11, Torsvik/Millesgården is 20.
  • Foreign consulate means the embassy and consulate category in Google Maps. Honorary consulates do not count.
  • A photo taken outside the station is taken at the platform or the stop pole when the zone has no station building.
  • Where a zone has no rail platform, I cannot answer the question is a valid answer to a train platform photo, and the hiders still draw.
  • Location sharing drops out in the tunnels. Curses that depend on where the seekers are use their last known position; if that is more than ten minutes old, the hiders may ask, and the seekers answer honestly.
  • The game language is fixed before the first round. It decides Curse of the Hidden Hangman and Curse of the Quill.
Deck

The deck

One hundred cards: 45 time bonuses, 25 powerups and 30 curses. Every curse is legal. A hider holds 6 cards unless a powerup raises the limit.

Time bonuses

The fixed cards carry three values, one per game size. We read the middle one.

Time trap5see below

Time bonuses cannot be played. They count only if they are in hand when the team is caught.

A per cent card is worth its share of the hiding time itself, worked out before any other bonus is added. Six of them in one hand pay six shares of the same clock, not a share of a share.

A time trap sits on one of our 252 stops and on no other station. The card leaves the hand as it is placed, and any time it wins is added at once. Passing through a station means travelling a line that runs through it.

Curses

  • Only one curse that blocks questions or transit runs at a time. The seekers say when they have cleared it.
  • A curse is discarded once it has been used.
  • A hider announces a powerup only when it affects the seekers.
